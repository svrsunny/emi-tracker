# Code of Conduct

EMI Tracker is a small, friendly project. Everyone taking part (in issues, pull requests and
discussions) is expected to:

- be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to open source or to programming;
- assume good intent, and keep criticism about the work, not the person;
- never share anyone's personal or financial information, including their own in public threads;
- accept that the maintainer makes the final decision about what is merged.

Harassment, insults, discrimination, sexual language or imagery, and publishing other people's
private information are not tolerated. Such comments are removed, and repeated or serious cases
lead to a ban from the repository.

To report a problem privately, use the contact route described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
